## The idea

Fear is not defeated by argument in “Schneller als Angst.” It is outrun. The lyric turns night, pain, cold, and heavy breath into resistance against which the will can accelerate, with every imperative—rise, run, burn, become—pushing the body farther into motion.

Fire begins as something hidden inside, then awakens, follows, and finally grows beyond anyone’s ability to contain it. That progression gives the trance-anthem structure its emotional logic: repetition is not standing still but gathering force on each return.

The destination remains extreme—the end of the world, even a fall into nothing—but the movement is toward morning and light. Courage here does not mean the absence of fear. It means becoming faster than fear can close the distance.
