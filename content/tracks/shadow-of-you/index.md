## The idea

Whispers through silver light; reach into endless night; a trace remains, perfume in the air. The shadow followed, fading dreams that will not undo; dance with the ghost in hollow silence. Moonlight on empty place—cold reflection, warm embrace; name breathed, not aloud; love never held, still surrendered to.

Dark-uplifting vocal trance thrives on this exact afterimage of presence. No release, no letting go: echo in the soul as permanent soft captivity.

Whether the ghost is dead, departed, or simply unreachable, the song’s ethics are clear. It prefers the dance with absence to the violence of forgetting—and it knows the cost of that preference.
