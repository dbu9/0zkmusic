## The idea

Martin Gore’s speaker addresses a listener who has already begun to judge. The verses mingle religious imagery with the language of a trial. Forbidden fruit makes temptation part of the evidence, while the references to judges and jurors show how quickly an audience can assume the authority to issue a verdict. The speaker claims pure intentions and asks for a test that would expose how much circumstance has shaped the case.

The refrain turns a familiar idiom into a concrete exercise. Step into the speaker’s shoes and the route comes with them, including the places where a person stumbles and the appointments that have to be kept. The challenge makes empathy a matter of inhabiting the same sequence of pressures. A female vocal places that challenge in another voice. The tango and djent framing also gives the central image of walking a useful musical counterpart, with a dance step supplying a way to think about constraint, balance, and the possibility of the next misstep.
