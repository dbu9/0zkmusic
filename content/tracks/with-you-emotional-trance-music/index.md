## The idea

A vow that outlasts biology. “i’m always with you, whatever happens”—through storm and silence, whisper in wind, shadow in light. Sorrows cradled, weight stolen from the soul, warmth in the bones, hand leading home. Dark-euphoric nocturnal vocal trance can turn companionship into cathedral architecture: presence as the only dogma.

Even when the world forgets a name, the speaker still sees “you as you are.” Trembling voice answered by song; shattered hope carried. And when last breath fades, love refuses to die—stars may burn out, yet “I’ll still be shining in your sky.” Whether one hears romantic partner, parent, or guardian spirit, the ethic is constancy beyond witness. The track’s lift is the sound of a promise that does not require the promiser to remain flesh.
