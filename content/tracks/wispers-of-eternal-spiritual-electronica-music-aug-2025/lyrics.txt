In silence I breathe,  
In darkness I see,  
The river within,  
The mirror of me.  

Lux aeternum, vita nova,  
Lux aeternum, anima sola.  

Shadows dissolve,  
Time slips away,  
Nothing remains,  
Yet all will stay.  

Touch of the unseen,  
Voice in the wind,  
The circle begins,  
The circle will end.  

Lux aeternum, vita nova,  
Echoes eternal, anima sola.
