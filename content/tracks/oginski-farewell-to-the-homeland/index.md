## The idea

The title places a private act of parting within the form of a processional dance. The stately triple meter of the polonaise implies that someone has to keep walking at a measured pace. In the context of a farewell, this regular forward motion can express a composure imposed by circumstance. The A-minor melody can be heard as everything that composure has to contain.

Djent lends itself to emphasizing the weight of each step. The connection to the polonaise lies in the importance both styles give to accent and to the listener’s sense of where the next beat falls. Here that rhythmic precision can bring out the conflict suggested by the title. Leaving requires progress in one direction precisely when the emotional force of the piece is pulling in the other.
