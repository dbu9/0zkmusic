You came in silence,  
Pale and weak,  
Whispered sorrow,  
Burned my speech.  

You lit the night,  
You cursed the day,  
Every song,  
You take away.  

I bleed in rhythm,  
Your gift, my chain,  
You breathe in fire,  
I drown in pain.  

Still I follow,  
Through the flame,  
Silent torment,  
Sacred name.
