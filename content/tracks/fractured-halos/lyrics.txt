Fractured Halos – Full Album (Instrumental Airy Trance | Minor Key | Immersive Soundscape)  

Dive into Fractured Halos, a weightless, melancholic journey through crystalline synth leads, breathy choirs, shimmering arpeggios, and vast evolving pads. No four-on-the-floor kicks, no pumping bass — just pure atmospheric immersion: distant metallic resonances, subtle granular textures, and buried field recordings drifting like signals from forgotten ruins under an endless night sky.  
Perfect for late-night contemplation, deep focus, stargazing, or emotional drift.
