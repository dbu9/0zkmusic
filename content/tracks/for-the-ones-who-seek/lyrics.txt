Not all will see  
Not all will feel  
A quiet spark  
Cuts through the steel  

No trumpet call  
No blazing sun  
A thread of fire  
For only one  

The dark is deep  
The crowd is blind  
But eyes that search  
Will surely find  

A pulse, unseen  
A distant gleam  
For hearts that ache  
For those who dream  

It will not burn  
To light the hall  
But in the void  
It speaks to all  

Some pass by  
Some never knew  
But to the few—  
The light shines through
