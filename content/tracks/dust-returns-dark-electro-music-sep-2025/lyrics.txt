I fear no death.  
I need no breath.  
A second gone,  
The void moves on.  

The pain is vain.  
It leaves no gain.  
Not my will,  
To suffer still.  

All must decay.  
All fades away.  
So why to cry?  
We live, we die.  

Believe or not.  
It changes not.  
The end will stay,  
The same old way.  

From dust begun,  
To dust undone.  
No need to care.  
No burden bear.  

Be calm, be free.  
Transcend with me.  
The night will pass.  
Return to dust.
