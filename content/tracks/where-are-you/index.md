## The idea

Empty streets that sound the same; every step a trace; night giving no clue. *Where are you now?* lost in echo, dreams crashing, still not letting go—burning through silence toward a light on a dark horizon.

Time dissolves; memories disobey; fire in the chest calls a lost desire. Even if the sky breaks, the chase continues through stars. Dark-uplifting trance is almost designed for this question: location unknown, motion certain. The beloved may be person, past self, or pure absence wearing a name.
