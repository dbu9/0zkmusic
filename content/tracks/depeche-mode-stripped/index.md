## The idea

“Stripped” begins with an invitation to spend a few hours outside the city. The lyric makes the metropolis intimate through a single sensory detail. The speaker can taste its fumes in a kiss. Even the tenderness that motivates the escape carries a trace of the place the lovers want to leave.

The refrain uses the body to describe a desire for exposure that extends to thought, speech, and grief. The speaker wants to hear the other person make a decision without the television and eventually asks to hear them cry. The repeated requests put pressure on the idea of freedom. Someone’s voice may emerge from the noise of the city and immediately find itself speaking for an audience of one. That ambiguity gives the nu metal and trumpet pairing room for two kinds of expression. The weight of the metal idiom can suggest pressure, and the breath behind a brass line can suggest the vulnerability the speaker keeps asking to hear.
