## The idea

When night is still, spirit is invited to sing; silence itself calls you “everything.” Fear of crying and falling is gently disarmed—the child within named as “the truth of all.” Path of light, shadows thinning at night’s edge; rivers of the heart as the place knowledge returns.

Trance here feels pastoral rather than martial: come back, know love’s voice, let the interior child unclench. I hear a soft corrective to hardness—not naive, exactly, but insistent that the center still has weather and water if one will walk inward.
