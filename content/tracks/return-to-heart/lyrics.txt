When the night is still,  
Let your spirit sing.  
When the silence calls,  
You are everything.  

Don’t be afraid,  
To cry, to fall.  
The child within,  
Is the truth of all.  

The path you walk,  
Is the path of light.  
Every shadow fades,  
At the edge of night.  

Come back to the heart,  
Where the rivers flow.  
In the voice of love,  
You will always know.
