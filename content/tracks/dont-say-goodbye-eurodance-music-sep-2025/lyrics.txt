I see the night is slowly falling,  
Your shadow fades into the sky.  
The words you whisper sound like sorrow,  
I can’t believe you’d say goodbye.  

The streets remember when we wandered,  
Our laughter echoing in the rain.  
If love was written in the starlight,  
Why must we let it slip away?  

Don’t say goodbye, don’t break my heart tonight,  
The flame still burns, it’s holding out for light.  
Through every storm, I’ll find you by my side,  
Don’t say goodbye, don’t say goodbye.  

Tomorrow’s dream is still before us,  
The morning sun will always rise.  
If we believe, then love will guide us,  
No need for tears, no last goodbyes.  

Don’t say goodbye, don’t break my heart tonight,  
The flame still burns, it’s holding out for light.  
Through every storm, I’ll find you by my side,  
Don’t say goodbye, don’t say goodbye.  

Don’t say goodbye…  
Just hold me close, and stay.
