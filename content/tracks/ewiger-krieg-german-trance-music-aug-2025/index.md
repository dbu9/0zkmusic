## The idea

War is eternal; silence passes. Hours break, blows call, the spiral turns, the forge glows, the sword wakes. “Sei bereit”—be ready—is both slogan and heartbeat. Trance and dark techno make perpetual conflict sound like architecture: drum as drum, iron that sings, banners that fall as a matter of course.

There is almost no interior psychology—only readiness, fire, threat. Peace does not count. One may hear critique, exaltation, or pure weather report from a world that cannot demobilize. The lyric’s brevity is its force: no negotiation, no aftermath, only the next beginning of the storm. In that sense “ewiger Krieg” is less narrative than climate, and the body is drafted by the beat before the mind consents.
