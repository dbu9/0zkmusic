## The idea

The title already argues with softness: ash becomes steel, past burns, names drop. Dark techno is the right chassis for this kind of will—cold streets, reset clocks, a body that owns the ground even when nobody is watching. The lyric keeps stripping alibis: no map, no clear way, only pulse and today.

“Kill the fear” lands less like a gym slogan than a night-shift commandment. Falls build spine; cracks become signs; screams turn to fuel. I hear someone refusing both nostalgia and audience, training alone under empty sound until fire is simply what runs in the veins.

There is little romance and less apology. The last claim—you were made to be more—is almost crude in its clarity, and the drums agree without ornament.
