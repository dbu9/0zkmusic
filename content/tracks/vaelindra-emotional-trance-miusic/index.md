## The idea

Romance in high tragic register. Names carved on fleeting sand, tides erasing vows; love torn; “ghost of fire where embers flew.” Bound by fate that deceives; stars bereaved; two wretched souls, one broken heart. The beloved’s voice becomes “a dirge in halls of stone,” each word a dagger; gold kisses turn to dust. Melodic club-trance energy can make ruin feel operatic—high energy carrying low fortune.

Time as villain writing doom in chill ink; one last dance where shadows weep; one last vow the grave shall keep. There is little everyday texture—only mythic scale, which may be the point: some losses refuse the language of therapy and demand marble and ash. Vaelindra (name as spell) holds the whole weather system: desire already half in the afterlife, still dancing while the tide finishes its work.
