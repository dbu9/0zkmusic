Be a little weaker, let the armor fall,  
Let your eyes remember, how it feels — to call.  
Let your voice be trembling, soft as broken glass,  
Let the storm within you finally pass.  

You don't lose — by bending,  
You don’t fade — by pain.  
Hearts that bleed together  
Are never the same.  

Be a little weaker —  
And love will find your name.  
Be a little slower —  
And time will lose its chain.  
Be a little kinder —  
The world will breathe again.  
Be a little weaker —  
And stronger you'll remain.  

All your shining victories, dust within your hand,  
All your silent tears have shaped this land.  
If the night is endless — stay, don’t turn away,  
In surrender’s fire, souls learn to pray.  

Close your eyes, dissolve in light,  
Weakness turns to flight.  
Forgive the world, forgive yourself,  
You are the wound, and you are the help.  

Be a little weaker —  
Let the burden fade.  
Be a little purer —  
Let the fear be laid.  
Be a little human —  
And gods will understand.  
Be a little weaker —  
And hold the world in hand.
