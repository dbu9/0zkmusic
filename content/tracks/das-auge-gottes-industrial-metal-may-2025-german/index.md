## The idea

“Wir sind das Auge—das niemals liegt.” The eye of God is not merciful; it is surveillance as sacrament. Cleanse, locate, one breath—one shot, one cut. Industrial metal’s aggression matches a voice that equates weakness with rot and light with the right to strike.

They scent, follow, capture; no step escapes, no word flies free. Later stanzas drop even borrowed divinity—“Kein Gott, kein Traum”—until only gaze and shield remain. Order lives, chaos dies, steel rules. Fist that never flees, will that never forgives, end that claims its hour.

I hear the terror of total certainty: a collective that has replaced prayer with targeting. Whether the “light” is justice or merely power wearing a holy mask is left for the listener’s nerves to decide.
