## The idea

Shakespearean heat with a dark-pop pulse. Stars pale before a sovereign light; the beloved is storm breaking night’s hush, “wrathful choir in love’s cathedral blaze.” Death-as-love accepted gladly: “thou alone art truth and tyranny.”

Eyes as daggers that make one bleed with grace; voice a dirge through vaulted stone where angels fear and devils rest. Servant and child in the same breath; wrath worn as sacred thorn; heart promised to thunderclaps even if heaven tears and hell collapses.

I hear sacred masochism of passion—organ agony, damnation sweet—melancholy nocturne for desire that wants no safer theology.
