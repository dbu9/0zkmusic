When stars did pale before thy sovereign light,  
My soul awoke to tremble at thy gaze.  
Thou art the storm that breaks the hush of night,  
The wrathful choir in love’s cathedral blaze.  

O strike the chord, let thunder kiss the sky!  
Thy name resounds in organed agony.  
If death be love, then let me gladly die—  
For thou alone art truth and tyranny.  

Thine eyes are daggers, yet I bleed with grace,  
Each wound a vow writ deep within my chest.  
Thy voice—a dirge sung through a vaulted place,  
Where angels fear, yet devils find their rest.  

O strike the chord, let thunder kiss the sky!  
Thy name resounds in organed agony.  
If death be love, then let me gladly die—  
For thou alone art truth and tyranny.  

In shadows’ breath, thy touch dost linger still,  
A ghostly hymn that bends my mortal will.  
Damnation sweet, salvation fierce and wild—  
In love, I am thy servant and thy child.  

O raise the veil, let heaven’s vault be torn!  
I’ll wear thy wrath like crown of sacred thorn.  
Though angels burn and hell itself collapse,  
My heart shall beat within thy thunderclaps.
