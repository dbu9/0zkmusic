## Lyrics

Shadows in the Neon is a tribute to the legendary Romanian Dance-Pop era of the late 2000s. Inspired by the melodic accordion hooks and smooth vocal style of Akcent and Edward Maya, this track brings that nostalgic Euro-House energy into 2026.  

As a programmer and PhD, I love exploring the intersection of precise logic and rhythmic emotion. If you enjoyed my recent Metal covers of classical works, you’ll recognize that same "epic" melodic structure here, just reimagined for the dance floor.
