## The idea

“Your time is short” initially sounds like a sentence passed on humanity, but the lyric makes a subtler distinction. Breath, face, soul, and form are not threatened; what cannot survive is certainty—the inherited assurance that God, truth, the self, and the world have already been understood.

The images give that philosophical collapse the scale of sacred architecture. Pillars carry silence, the organ carries the All, and an invisible fall travels through the vault while dead angels cross empty heavens. Every note becomes judgment because music reaches the place where explanation has failed.

This is apocalypse in its older sense: an unveiling. High becomes deep, order flees, and speech without a mouth becomes prayer. The terror lies not in annihilation, but in being forced to remain present while the ideas that once made existence feel stable lose their power.
