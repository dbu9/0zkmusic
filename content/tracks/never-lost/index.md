## The idea

Child of fire, born to rise; echo where silence dies; age as dust, dream that stays. Wonder carried fierce and true until darkness fades and spark breaks through. Flame eternal, never tame; light surviving inside the name; chains of time unable to hold the core—the child within forevermore.

Dark and uplifting trance can sound like motivational poster music if the writing is thin. Here the insistence is almost pagan: genius rises, shadows fall, power endless and unsealed. Enthusiasm treated as sacred fever rather than as self-help.

I hear a hymn to the unkillable interior self—the part that outshines adulthood’s grey. Whether you find it true or merely desired, the track sings as if remembering were a form of rebellion.
