I built my dreams in silver lines,  
Beneath the calm of gentler times.  
The fields were wide, the skies were clear,  
But fate was whispering near.  

We wait for happiness to come,  
But sorrow finds us, one by one.  
The best-laid hopes, they fade away,  
Like stars that die before the day.  

I saw the dawn, I called it mine,  
But night returned before the shine.  
The seed I planted turned to rain,  
And joy was shadowed by the pain.  

We wait for happiness to come,  
But sorrow finds us, one by one.  
The best-laid hearts will break and bend,  
And dreams go lost around the bend.  

Yet still we build, though all decays,  
Still light the fire against the haze.  
For even pain — its fleeting breath —  
Reminds us we’re alive in death.  

We wait for happiness to come,  
But sorrow finds us, one by one.  
The field lies bare, the sky is torn,  
Yet still, we sow — though hearts are worn.
