## The idea

The words do not resolve into a dictionary language. Elohira, repeated; syllables that behave like names, prayers, or places. Vocal trance often uses such glossolalia as emotional pigment—uplift and drive without the burden of a plot you could summarize.

What remains is atmosphere as meaning: call and answer, crescendo, fade to whisper. If Elohira is a person, a goddess, or simply a sound that fits the chord, the track does not need to decide. The mouth shapes devotion; the kick supplies conviction.
