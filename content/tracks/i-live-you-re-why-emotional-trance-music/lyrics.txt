You move like light.  
I fade from sight.  
You never see.  
But still I reach.  

You laugh. I freeze.  
You shine with ease.  
You feel no flame.  
I burn the same.  

I'd give it all.  
Just one small call.  
You look ahead.  
I'm left for dead.  

The gap is wide.  
But hope won't hide.  
Each day, I try.  
I live. You’re why.
