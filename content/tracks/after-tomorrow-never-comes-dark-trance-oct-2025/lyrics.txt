We said — not today, there’s time,  
We’ll love, we’ll call, we’ll cross that line.  
But night arrives, the clocks dissolve,  
And nothing’s left to still resolve.  

The hours fall like broken glass,  
Dreams fade before they come to pass.  
The lips are mute, the hands grow cold,  
The stories die — they stay untold.  

We always wait, we always plan,  
And time just laughs — a cruel man.  
The stars look down, they do not speak,  
Their light is old, their fire weak.  

Tomorrow’s ghost walks through the room,  
It whispers softly, sealing doom:  
“You had your chance, but now it’s gone —  
You lived for later, and later’s none.”  

No after, no dawn, no second run,  
The sun stands still, the world undone.  
Forgive the time you failed to live —  
The dark takes all you didn’t give.  

Don’t wait for peace, don’t wait for sign,  
The pulse is fading down the line.  
Now or never — it’s all the same,  
The clock burns slow, it speaks your name.
