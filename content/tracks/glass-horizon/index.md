## The idea

Neon folds the body into night; the self dissolves into the screen. “Pixel rain across my skin,” doors opening inward, flesh-noise falling away into endless sky. Synthwave’s nocturnal chill loves this borderland where code becomes intimacy and data is “pure and deep,” a lucid dream that refuses to end.

Losing the name is almost a relief—“patterns bloom, they look the same.” Escape and nearness tangle: through the wires, “I feel you near.” Perhaps the horizon is glass because it is both window and mirror, barrier and display. The song does not mourn embodiment so much as stage a soft vanishing into circuitry, where hidden streams carry echoes and the night feels more real than the room left behind.
