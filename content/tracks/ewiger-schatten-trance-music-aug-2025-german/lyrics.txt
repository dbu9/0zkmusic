*Eternal Shadow*  

You are the shadow  
A faded light  
Forgotten dreams  
Escape my sight  

An echo in rain  
A cry in the night  
The flame has died  
The soul takes flight  

Shattered visions  
Scattered in wind  
The voices are silent  
Like ash they rescind  

What once was alive  
Is dust in the air  
What once was hope  
Turns into despair  

You are the shadow  
That always stays  
The past eternal  
That drives my ways
