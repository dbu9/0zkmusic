## The idea

“Du bist der Schatten / Verblasstes Licht”—address to a presence that is absence. Echo in rain, cry in night, flame out, soul awake: the paradox is that extinction of the outer fire seems to wake something inward. Shattered images in wind, hope turned to dust and dream; the shadow remains and *drives*.

Uplifting vocal trance against such ash-language creates a beautiful friction. The past is not healed; it becomes motor. Eternal shadow as companion rather than enemy—melancholy that refuses to stop moving.
