## The idea

Love as weak radio through static. A name fading, heartbeat turned to distance, lights under water, voices in rain—walking the border “between memory and pain.” Then the call from “black blue,” soft and far, pulling through. “I follow / The signal in the dark” is both romance and survival tactic. Dark-driving vocal trance thrives on that pursuit: uplift as tracking beam.

Cities to embers, windows without glow; no questions for the stars—only going “where shadows go.” Silver under thunder, fire under scars; when sky fractures, the beloved is heard in the stars. The surrender list—take fear, fever, road, receiver, ache, spark—asks to be left “only with the dark,” which is paradoxically where the signal lives. Presence remains “unspoken”: connection without clean resolution, fidelity as endless following through ocean and resistance.
