## The idea

After Gorky’s stormy petrel: black waves, torn sky, frozen world, thunder circling. German trance with Berlin-school atmosphere paints the bird that screams and wakes the night—wings burning, wind obeying, soul searching through dark silence.

No false peace left; from storm and chaos, sun will be. The petrel is not victim of weather but its herald. Stumme Klippen, das Nichts nah—then the cry.

I hear revolutionary weather more than personal mood: the one who flies into the storm as the true measure of courage.
