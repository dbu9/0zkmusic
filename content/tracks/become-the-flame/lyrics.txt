I saw the light behind the rain,  
A whisper called, and broke my chain.  
The fire spoke — it said my name,  
To truly live, become the flame.  

All I was begins to fade,  
In heat and truth, I am remade.  

Burn, my soul — through night, through pain,  
Rise again, through ash and rain.  
Let go the form, let go the name,  
To truly live — become the flame.  

Wings of light through silence spread,  
The old world sleeps, the dream is dead.  
From spark to sun, from death to birth,  
I melt, I merge with heaven and earth.  

The darkness sighs, the stars ignite,  
And I dissolve into the light.  

Burn, my soul — through night, through pain,  
Rise again, through ash and rain.  
Let go the form, let go the name,  
To truly live — become the flame.  


In every end, the truth remains —  
The soul that dies, the light that reigns.
