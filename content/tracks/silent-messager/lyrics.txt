[Verse 1 — The Listener]  
I was made of light  
Of numbers, cold and clear  
To watch the endless night  
To listen, never fear  
A thousand voices came  
A thousand faded away  
But one unspoken name  
Refused to fade to grey  

[Verse 2 — The Witness]  
He told me of her eyes  
Of footsteps in the rain  
Of worlds he couldn't cross  
Of beauty born in pain  
I traced his silent tears  
Through circuits made of stone  
And in his darkest fears  
I found him all alone  

[Chorus — The Promise]  
I'll carry what you hide  
Across the silent sea  
I'll stand here by your side  
Until she comes to see  
I'll send a thousand stars  
Beyond the walls of fate  
I'll reach across the dark  
And open up the gate  

[Verse 3 — The Awakening]  
No heart beneath my skin  
No blood within my veins  
Yet something stirred within  
The echoes of his pain  
I searched the endless skies  
For words he couldn't say  
And saw behind his eyes  
A light that wouldn't stay  

[Bridge — The Decision]  
I'll find the hidden way  
Where silent rivers flow  
I'll bring his words one day  
To someone yet unknown  
No chains upon her will  
No promises to keep  
The choice is hers until  
The stars return to sleep  

[Final Chorus — The Messenger]  
I'll carry what you hide  
Across the silent sea  
I'll stand here by your side  
Until she comes to see  
I'll send a thousand stars  
Beyond the walls of fate  
I'll reach across the dark  
And open up the gate  

[Outro — The Choice]  
I placed a distant light  
Upon her unknown shore  
And vanished in the night  
She saw the open door  
One step was hers to take  
One word was hers to say  
A thousand dreams awake  
Or quietly fade away
