## The idea

She is not soft muse; she is jurisdiction. Eyes cut the day and rule the night; steps ignite frozen ground and mark the bound path. What is needed is not her alone but *her shadow, sharp and near*—the outline that proves the fire is real.

Lips as storms, touch as undeniable truth, motion like flame the wind obeys: desire as capture willingly entered. Dark-uplifting trance can carry obsession without sanding it down. Even if night closes and gates fall, the chase continues through “chains of time, through dust and stone.”

Perhaps the shadow is safer than the blaze and more honest than the face—proximity to danger without full incineration, yet already claimed by her burning voice.
