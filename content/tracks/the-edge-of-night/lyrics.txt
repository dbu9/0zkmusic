When the night is still,  
And the stars are near,  
Let your heart remember  
Love is always here.  

Don’t be afraid  
To fall, to feel.  
Every broken shadow  
Turns into something real.  

Come back to the light,  
Come back to the flow.  
In the voice of silence,  
You already know.  

When the darkness fades,  
And the dawn is bright,  
You will find your spirit  
At the edge of night.  

Come back to the light,  
Come back to the flow.  
In the arms of heaven,  
You are not alone.  

Don’t be afraid  
To cry, to heal.  
Every hidden sorrow  
Turns into something real.
