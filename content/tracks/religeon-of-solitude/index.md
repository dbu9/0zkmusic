## The idea

Solitude as creed: no idol, no shrine, night as guide, power growing free of the crowd’s defense. Trance drive makes the isolation feel chosen rather than merely suffered—alone, yet not confined; a vast unbroken mind.

Their voices fade to dust; trust lies in shadows; the deeper call sits beyond everyone’s reach. Belief happens in darkness; breathing happens in solitude. The misspelled “religeon” in the title almost fits—a private orthodoxy that never needed a temple.

I hear neither pure arrogance nor pure wound. It is the sound of someone who found a god-shaped silence and stayed.
