## The idea

Silent whispers, a distant flame, shadows on silver ground: the night is full of almost-voices and unfinished returns. The plea is simple and old—take me where moonlight falls, hold me close, don’t fade—while winds cry “tales of sorrow, long ago,” frozen rivers and weeping stars keeping the melancholy honest.

Emotional trance and melodic progressive colors can lift without lying; the uplift here feels like a hand offered inside the mist and empty halls. “Lost in echoes, never found” may be the truest line—desire for presence that knows how easily dawn, or distance, dissolves what the dark made intimate.
