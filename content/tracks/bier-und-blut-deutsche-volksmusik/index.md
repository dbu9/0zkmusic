## The idea

Morning is raw; drums call; steel and oath are present—and still the honest motor of the march is golden beer in the track. Bier und Blut: fight and laugh, mug in hand, drum in the body. The captain is dead, but his cup is full; one last sip for the missing friend. Courage, the lyric insists with a grin, grows where hops decide.

Under nocturnal electronic weather the drinking song becomes half-ritual, half-absurd liturgy. War may take them; the rausch might finish what steel does not—and death, in this swagger, still owes respect to a proper Saufgelage. Brotherhood is sworn not only to blade but to raised krug.

I hear it less as military propaganda than as dark carnival: mortality accepted, then mocked, then toasted. The steel still shines; the tankard still rises; the joke is the armor.
