In the silence before the storm,  
I feel the pulse, the lightning form.  
Frozen breath, electric eyes,  
Two worlds meet — and crystallize.  

You burn — I freeze,  
Still we dance in symmetry.  
You strike — I glow,  
Between the thunder and the snow.  

Lightning and frost, collide in the night,  
Hearts of the storm, alive in the light.  
Frozen desire, electric and lost,  
We are the echo — lightning and frost.  

Flames beneath the icy glass,  
Memories rise, shadows pass.  
Every spark, a whisper loud,  
Falling rain — or silver cloud.  

Touch me once — I turn to flame,  
Say my name — and melt again.  
The storm remembers what we forgot,  
Lightning and frost… lightning and frost.  

Cold fire, warm snow,  
We fade where the wild winds go.  
Two souls, never crossed,  
Still shining — lightning and frost.
