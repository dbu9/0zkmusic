You were here —  
then thinner than a thought,  
a shape the light forgot,  
a sentence breaking off before it starts.  

Your shadow learned to drift,  
to lift, to blur, to split,  
I reach for what you meant,  
not what you did.  

I don’t ask you to remain,  
I don’t pull you back again,  
every step you take away  
repeats your name.  

Not goodbye, not a lie,  
just a distance multiplying.  
You don’t vanish — you unwind,  
you leave time behind.  

Like smoke in the light —  
visible, untouchable,  
close enough to hurt,  
too soft to hold.  

Like breath in the night,  
warm for a second,  
gone before I know  
what I was owed.  

You move through me slow,  
then you go —  
not erased, just thinned,  
like a promise breathing in.  

I tried to keep the sound you made,  
the way you stayed but never stayed,  
the way your silence filled the room  
and left no trace.  

I don’t chase, I don’t accuse,  
every answer learns to lose.  
What we were was almost true —  
almost you.  

You don’t fall, you don’t remain,  
you just change without a name.  
No collision, no repair,  
still — you’re there.  

Smoke in the light,  
curling, untying,  
learning the shape  
of goodbye.  

Smoke in my lungs,  
truth on my tongue,  
real for a breath,  
then undone.  

Don’t tell me to save you,  
don’t tell me to stay —  
some things only exist  
while they fade.  

Still in the air…  
still aware…  
still not gone…  
just not there.  

Smoke in the light…  
soft as a scar…  
you were real —  
for as long as you are.
