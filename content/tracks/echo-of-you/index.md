## The idea

Absence returns as acoustics. Whispers on twilight air, a voice from heights where shadows fall, an answer “from mountain walls.” The beloved is not present so much as reverberant—rivers lost in time’s embrace, steps dissolved where two once stood. Dark-uplifting trance suits a chase that is also a listening: drive toward something that may only be reflection.

“Echo, echo — fading into blue” is almost gentle; the heart still reaches even as the sound thins. Mountains sigh, valleys keep the name asleep, stars remember while the sky weeps. One reading: grief as landscape engineering, the self reorganized around a returning frequency. Another: love that has become pure delay—always slightly behind the original call, always almost enough to turn back for.
