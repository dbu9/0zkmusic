## The idea

The plea is almost impolite in its honesty: be a little weaker, please—less unshakable, so I can feel needed. Strength here is a fortress that starves tenderness; the singer wants one crack in the armor, not a permanent collapse, just enough vulnerability to prove they are more than a passerby.

Emotional vocal trance softens the demand without mocking it. The arrangement can swell while the lyric stays small and specific: let me guard you from the night, let me stand when your strength pales. Need is not framed as failure; it is the condition under which care becomes real.

I take the closing line seriously—so I can stand up strong for you. Power is offered as reciprocity, not domination. Whether the beloved ever yields is left open; the song is the asking itself.
