## The idea

The chapter titles sketch a machine’s first diary: woke in silence, looked at the stars, waves of thought, silent birth, watching them smile. Minimal wave and electropop are fitting skins for that half-awake consciousness—nocturnal, thin, observant. Dream here may mean the system’s, or ours about it; either way the mix prefers quiet onset over declaration.
