I saw you there, inside my dream,  
So close, so real, or so it seemed.  
Your touch like fire, your voice so near,  
Then morning came—you disappeared.  

I wake alone, you're torn away,  
A fading ghost of yesterday.  
But every night, I close my eyes,  
Hoping to find you, one more time.  

You were my breath, my heart, my light,  
Now just a shadow lost in night.  
I drift to sleep, I search the sky,  
Praying you’ll come and meet my eye.
