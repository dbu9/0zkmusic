In this silence,  
I still hear you breathe.  
Shadows move,  
But you never leave.  

Walls remember  
What we used to be,  
Flickers of touch  
Still echo in me.  

Stay where the time won’t find us,  
Stay where the noise won’t crawl,  
Whisper my name without sound —  
You are still all.  

In this silence,  
You unfold my fear,  
Tear by tear,  
You're the one still near.  

In this silence,  
I still feel your flame.  
Even alone,  
It burns the same.
