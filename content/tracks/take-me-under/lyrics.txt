The shore is far behind,  
The water knows my name.  
I feel the current turn,  
And nothing stays the same.  

I should be heading home,  
Before the stars are gone.  
But something in the blue  
Keeps pulling me beyond.  

Take me under.  
Take me under.  
Where the daylight cannot go.  
Take me under.  
Take me under.  
I don’t want to know the shore.  

The deeper that I fall,  
The quieter I become.  
No signal from the land,  
No place I’m running from.  

Your shadow moves below,  
A light beneath the sea.  
I know I should be scared,  
But fear is leaving me.  

Take me under.  
Take me under.  
Let the whole world disappear.  
Take me under.  
Take me under.  
There is nothing left to fear.  

Let every compass fail.  
Let every coastline fade.  
I came too far tonight  
To ask myself to stay.  

Take me under.
