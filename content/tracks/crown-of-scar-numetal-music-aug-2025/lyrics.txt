I fall but I rise,  
With fire in my eyes.  
The pain becomes my light,  
I’m stronger in the fight.  

I break but I repair,  
No weight I cannot bear.  
Each scar becomes my crown,  
No force can pull me down.  

I rest, the night is mine,  
I wake, the stars still shine.  
No thought will make me yield,  
My fate is on this field.  

I push, the storm obeys,  
I run through endless days.  
No wall can make me stay,  
I carve my boundless way.  

I burn, my flame won’t fade,  
Through trials I have stayed.  
No chain can hold my sky,  
My will will never die.
