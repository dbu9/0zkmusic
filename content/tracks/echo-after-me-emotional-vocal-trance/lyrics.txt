𝑳𝒚𝒓𝒊𝒄𝒔 🎶🎶  

I walk through days like borrowed threads,  
Stitched in silence, frayed in dread.  
No child, no shrine, no golden breath—  
Just songs that hum beneath my death.  

A whisper carved in data streams,  
Forgotten chords and fractured dreams.  
My name may rot, my face may flee,  
But echoes sing what's left of me.  

They’ll find no bones, no final plea—  
Just melodies that used to be.
