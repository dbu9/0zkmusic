𝐿𝑖𝑘𝑒, 𝐶𝑜𝑚𝑚𝑒𝑛𝑡, 𝑆𝑢𝑏𝑠𝑐𝑟𝑖𝑏𝑒  ❤️🙏  


𝑳𝒚𝒓𝒊𝒄𝒔 🎶🎶  

They gather like warm weather,  
Eyes bright with easy flame.  
I float behind the laughter,  
Unspoken, without name.  

The air is thin with voices,  
Too light for me to hold.  
I drift beneath their brightness,  
A quiet growing cold.  

There’s water in the room,  
But no one sees the tide.  
They speak in open sunlight—  
I’m folded up inside.  

They speak of skies and stories,  
Of places they belong.  
Their words are woven music—  
But I don’t know the song.  

I nod and shape my silence  
To match their open air.  
But all the while I’m fading,  
And no one sees me there.  

There’s water in the room,  
But no one feels the rise.  
I smile like nothing’s breaking  
While drowning in disguise.  

I don’t reach for rescue,  
Don’t cry or leave a trace.  
I just become the silence  
That lives behind the face.  

So let them keep on shining,  
Let laughter fill the space—  
I’ll sit inside the quiet,  
The one they can’t replace.  

There’s water in the room,  
And I have grown so small.  
But I will keep on breathing  
Where no one sees at all.
