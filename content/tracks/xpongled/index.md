## The idea

The face on the cover holds your gaze while everything around it seems to be moving. Yellow and black curl into each other, and the lines around the eyes carry those curves onto the face itself. Even the lettering looks ready to break apart. The longer you look, the harder it gets to say where the portrait ends and the motion around it begins.

That tension offers a way into the instrumental. Trance gives the ear a point of return, while the psybient side makes room to wander. A recurring pulse can feel reassuring early on and strange a few minutes later, simply because your attention has moved elsewhere. “Xpongled” leaves that movement up to the listener. You can keep your place in the rhythm and still find yourself looking, as with the cover, at something you could have sworn was in the background.
