## The idea

Grief after a missed call that never gets returned in this life. “My friend, you called, I let it ring”—tomorrow was a lie; pain was not silent; the speaker walked away “too blind, too cold.” Love and hate tangle; now only echoes keep the name. Trap’s dark aggression can make regret hit like confession under a streetlight.

The chorus is reunion deferred past death: calm, no more fight, “wait for me—we’ll reunite.” Self-blame catalogs every stupid argument while the other was “losing will to write.” Smoke, light, tears, rain replay; breath would be traded if trade were possible. The song does not absolve. It holds the quiet the dead take, and promises a road that is tight, soon, and only imaginary until it isn’t—or never is, except as music.
