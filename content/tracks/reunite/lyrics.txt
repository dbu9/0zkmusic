My friend, you called, I let it ring,  
I thought tomorrow still would bring.  
You drowned in pain I didn’t see,  
you screamed for help — not silently.  

I walked away, too blind, too cold,  
your fire turned to ash and mold.  
I hated you, yet loved the same,  
now only echoes speak your name.  

Now you’re calm, no more to fight,  
no more shadows, endless night.  
No more words we twist and break,  
just the quiet that you take.  
I’ll follow soon, the road is tight,  
wait for me — we’ll reunite.  

I blame myself for what I missed,  
for every call, for every list  
of stupid things we used to fight,  
while you were losing will to write.  

The smoke, the light, the tears, the rain,  
they all replay inside my brain.  
I’d trade my breath to make it right,  
but you’re already past the light.  

Now you’re calm, no more to fight,  
no more dreams that end in fright.  
I’ll keep your name inside my night —  
until we meet,  
and reunite.
