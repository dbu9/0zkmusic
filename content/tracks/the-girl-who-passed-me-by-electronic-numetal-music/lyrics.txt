𝑳𝒚𝒓𝒊𝒄𝒔 🎶🎶  

She walked alone,  
In fading light.  
One look was fire,  
Then came the night.  

I stayed in place,  
Too scared to call.  
She slipped away,  
Like leaves in fall.  

I search the crowd,  
Through endless rain.  
Her face is gone,  
But not the pain.  

Will time be kind?  
Will paths align?  
Or was she just  
A ghost in mine?
