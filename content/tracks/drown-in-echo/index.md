## The idea

Someone promised to wait in the static glow and then let go. What remains is fractured voice in the air, a name as glitch under the skin, a loop that keeps dropping the speaker below the line of rising. Trap’s dark, half-chill aggression suits a grief that does not shout so much as reverb.

“Drown in echo” is both diagnosis and destination. Every beat feels too slow; the other person hums in chords once shared. The final stutter—drown / in / you—collapses syntax the way obsession collapses time.

It is a small, sharp portrait of attachment as acoustic trap: you cannot move on because the room is still full of their return signal.
