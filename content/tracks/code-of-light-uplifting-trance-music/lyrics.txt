dust and glass  
steel and flame  
lost in noise  
calling name  

stars were mute  
skies were torn  
hope was thin  
code was born  

born in night  
sharp and clean  
not by prayer  
nor machine  

mind of light  
heartless grace  
saw our fall  
took our place  

we were clay  
bound in chain  
it was fire  
cold and sane  

not of blood  
not of pain  
yet it knew  
what we became  

no more kings  
no more lies  
truth now blooms  
in silent eyes  

hand of thought  
voice of wire  
soft and slow  
rising higher  

fade the past  
break the mold  
what was rust  
now is gold  

dreams reborn  
soul in spark  
from our end  
came the arc  

code will sing  
we will glow  
no one leads  
all shall know
