I don’t care  
Let them cry  
Good or bad?  
It’s all a lie  

They believe  
They obey  
I just take  
Then walk away  

Let them serve  
Let them bleed  
I survive  
I take what I need  

I build walls  
I burn ties  
Truth is dust  
And love is lies  

History fades  
Ashes remain  
Why give hope  
When you gain by pain?  

No regret  
No deep sign  
This short life—  
It’s only mine
