## The idea

Walking through the silence where a voice used to live; laughter’s echo already far. Night remembers what morning will not speak. Warmth to ash, promise to grief—dark pop’s melancholic economy, few words, each one placed.

Hands close on shadows; love was a river that cannot be crossed again. Sorrow is asked to play the final tone: lost devotion as music, the heart left alone as the last instrument. No rescue plot. Only the song admitting it is ending.
