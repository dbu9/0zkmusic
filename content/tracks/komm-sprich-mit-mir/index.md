## The idea

Come, speak—in the storm of time, where shadows move and no one remains. Wind retells old guilt; hearts that love and hearts that endure. Night deep, moon clear, a call both far and near; in silence a quiet light burns with no beginning or end in sight. Rain, oak tree calling for loyalty, ancient vow glowing past death and pain.

German vocal trance can carry this folkloric gravity without irony. “Die Welt vergeht — das Wort besteht” is almost a credo: world fades, word stays, as long as the heart still understands the other.

I take it as a summons across absence—half love song, half invocation—asking for speech as the only bridge that outlasts the weather of history.
