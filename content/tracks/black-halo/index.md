## The idea

Snow-eyes, flame-hands, a kiss that taught pain: the imagery is gothic without needing a cathedral. Progressive vocal trance wraps the heartbreak in nocturnal euphoria, so “black halo, fall on me” can sound like both curse and invitation.

Burning the ghost you used to be is a hard wish—renewal that still requires the old wound. The voice stays near as a knife of fear; the scar worn like a dead star that somehow still gives light. No dawn behind those eyes, no prayer where the beloved decays: the song does not negotiate a happier ending.

What it offers instead is surviving inside the radiance of damage. Call my name inside the rain; break the heart alive again. Darkness and lift share the same pulse.
