No crown, no name,  
No stolen flame.  
The bloodline calls,  
The false one falls.  

With roots or lies,  
The spirit dies.  
The past is near,  
Choose truth or fear.  

The traitors speak,  
Their voices weak.  
No mask, no creed,  
Can plant the seed.  

By deeds you rise,  
By deeds you shine.  
No words, no guise,  
The fire is mine.
