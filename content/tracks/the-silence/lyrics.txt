I came back to the coffin.  
I laid my ear against the wood.  
I had asked for quiet.  
Quiet was what I found.  

No whisper rose to meet me.  
No breath moved underneath.  
The thing I buried  
had kept its word.  

[Verse 2 — Dark, distant]  
I spoke the notes I remembered.  
They came back cold and clear.  
Nothing stirred beneath the skin.  
Nothing answered in the dark.  

The door was still an outline.  
The way behind it gone.  
I stayed there listening  
to what would not return.  

[Chorus — Crushing, melodic]  
Stay silent. Speak to me.  
Stay buried. Answer.  
I am the one who shut the lid,  
the one who listens now.  

I wanted peace.  
I have the peace.  
Don’t leave me with it.  
Don’t come back.  

[Bridge — Whispered, almost spoken]  
I buried you to be free.  
The freedom holds.  
You do not call.  
I call and you do not answer.  

The pain is gone.  
The absence stays.  
I press my ear harder  
and hear only the wood.  

[Final Chorus — Heavy, descending]  
Stay silent. Speak to me.  
Stay buried. Answer.  
I am the one who shut the lid,  
the one who listens now.  

I wanted peace.  
I have the peace.  
Don’t leave me with it.  
Don’t come back.  

[Outro — Instruments dying, barely audible]  
I called once more.  
Nothing answered.  
I stayed until the light  
had left.  

Then silence.  
I did not rise.
