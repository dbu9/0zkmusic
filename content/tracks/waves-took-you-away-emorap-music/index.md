## The idea

Beach myth in a single night: Aphrodite at the waterline, gold dust hair, tide pulling speech under. Eyes that look through; footprints erased; forever glimpsed in a laugh and then denied. Emorap’s aggressive drive keeps the romanticism from floating free—it has grit, notebook of past names, heart “dripping red.”

Chasing shadows, waking from a dream too fast, shoreline walks for a sign that does not come. Tide as the other person’s nature: gone without warning, ghost lingering. Another life under the same sky is allowed as consolation and immediately deferred. The waves take; the speaker stays yelling at echoes.
