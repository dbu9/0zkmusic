## The idea

Perhaps the lyric means less “we” as a crowd than “we” as a single body discovered only in reflection. A thousand eyes, one flame: multiplicity reduced to a shared heat, glass turned into an ocean so memory can move as water rather than as argument. I hear a quiet pantheism without dogma—the sacred note is not an anthem but a hum you fall into.

Trance suits that dissolution. Uplifting lines and driving pulse do what the words already ask: dissolve thought, let sound briefly harden into form, then return to air. The refusal of worship and prayer is almost modest. Union here is ordinary and immense at once—strangers keeping time with the same light for the length of a night, then vanishing without needing a name.
