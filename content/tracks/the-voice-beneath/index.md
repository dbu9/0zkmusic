## The idea

“The Voice Beneath” turns the man’s attempt to put an end to his longing into a burial. In the series, he sees the woman often enough for the feeling to recur, yet she is unaware of the life it has acquired in him. The lyric gives his love a body that can breathe, suffer, and call through the soil. Its innocence makes the decision to bury it harder. Its only offense is to have stayed alive.

### The hand and the ear

The refrain gives the man two roles at the same time. He is the hand that closed the coffin and the ear pressed to the ground. Those two parts of the body make concrete a division he cannot talk himself out of. He wants the feeling to remain buried and to give a sign that it survives. The paired commands ask for silence and sound in the same breath. When he imagines opening the lid and bleeding, then leaving it closed and making the feeling bleed in his place, the metaphor shows how much of his own life is still bound up with what he is trying to suppress.

### The whisper that stops him

The bridge briefly distinguishes the woman, whose heart he never knew, from the love to which he has been speaking. The only dialogue he hears comes from the feeling itself. At first he walks on when the voice asks if he is still there. At the end it asks again and he stops. That change in action is the song’s quietest disclosure. He has put distance between himself and the grave, but a question from beneath the earth can still determine his next step.
