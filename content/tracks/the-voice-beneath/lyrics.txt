[Verse 1 — Low, restrained]  
I dug a narrow grave.  
I laid my love inside.  
It had committed no crime.  
It was only alive.  
I nailed the coffin shut.  
I covered it with clay.  
I called the silence mercy  
And turned away.  

[Verse 2 — Dark, distant]  
I walked across the grave.  
A whisper reached my feet:  
"Are you still there?"  
I pretended not to hear.  
She never knew it lived.  
I knew its every breath.  
I kept on walking  
Above what wasn't dead.  

[Chorus — Crushing, melodic]  
Stay buried. Stay alive.  
Be silent. Make a sound.  
I am the hand that shut the lid,  
The ear against the ground.  
If I open it, I bleed.  
If I leave it shut, you do.  
Don't call to me.  
Don't fade away.  

[Bridge — Whispered, almost spoken]  
I never knew her heart.  
She never knew of you.  
I buried you to live —  
Not because you weren't true.  
You never asked to suffer.  
You only wished to stay.  
I was the one who buried you.  
I was the one who prayed.  

[Final Chorus — Heavy, descending]  
Stay buried. Stay alive.  
Be silent. Make a sound.  
I am the hand that shut the lid,  
The ear against the ground.  
Don't call to me.  
Don't fade away.  

[Outro — Instruments dying, barely audible]  
I walked until the whisper  
Was too faint to hear.  
Then silence.  
Then, beneath the earth:  
"Are you there?"  
I stopped.
