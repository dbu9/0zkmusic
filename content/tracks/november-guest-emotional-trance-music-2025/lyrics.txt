𝑳𝒚𝒓𝒊𝒄𝒔 🎶🎶  

My sorrow comes to visit me,  
She sees the rain and falling leaves,  
And says these days are full of grace—  
The beauty found in quiet space.  

She loves the trees so bare and old,  
The wind that cuts, the air that's cold.  
She walks the muddy pasture path,  
And smiles beneath the sky’s grey wrath.  

Her joy won’t let me turn away,  
She speaks, and I feel drawn to stay.  
She’s glad the birds have flown the skies,  
She loves the mist that softly lies.  

Her simple cloak of woolen grey  
Turns silver in the foggy day.  
She sees such beauty all around,  
In lifeless trees and soggy ground.  

She thinks I cannot see these things,  
The peace the empty landscape brings.  
She asks me why I’m blind to this,  
And why I do not share her bliss.  

But I have known since long ago  
The calm before the winter snow.  
Yet words can’t make her understand—  
It means more praised by her own hand.
