[Verse 1]  

A figure.  
Far away.  
A silent walk.  
A fleeting shape.  

A second.  
Less.  
A fraction  
Of a breath.  

I knew.  

It was you.  

[Verse 2]  

Faces dissolve in air.  
Names fade to silence.  
A thousand people  
Pass like rain.  

Another place.  
Another light.  
Another distance.  
Another sight.  

And yet—  

I knew.  

[Chorus]  

It was you.  
Before the thought.  
Before my eyes  
Could understand.  

It was you.  
No chance.  
No doubt.  
No reason  
To explain.  

The empty space  
Was full of you.  

And I knew.  

[Verse 3]  

Not memory.  
Instinct.  

I barely know you.  
Yet I know.  

From any distance.  
From any angle.  
In any crowd.  

You.  

A warning.  
A miracle.  

[Chorus]  

It was you.  
Before the thought.  
Before my eyes  
Could understand.  

It was you.  
No chance.  
No doubt.  
No reason  
To explain.  

The empty space  
Was full of you.  

And I knew.  

[Bridge]  

Who knew?  

Not my eyes.  
They were late.  

Who spoke?  

No voice.  
No sound.  

An answer  
Without a question.  

A certainty  
Without a cause.  

[Final Verse ]  

The distance  
Was still there.  

The figure  
Still a shadow.  

The face  
Still unknown.  

But somewhere  
Deep inside—  

A bell  
Without a sound  
Rang once.  

It knew.  
Before I did.  

[Outro ]  

What is sight  
When knowledge  
Comes before?  

What is distance  
When presence  
Needs no proof?  

The mind  
Demands a reason.  

The heart  
Has none.  

And my eyes—  

They came too late.
