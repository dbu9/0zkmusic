## The idea

A shore walk becomes a long address to absence. Wind, foam, shells underfoot—ordinary coastal furniture—carry a name that will not stay still. The sea does not answer; it redistributes the question. Vocal trance, here, is less about a peak than about a pulse that keeps walking when location fails.

Moonlight “weeps” on sand with a slight excess of pathos that the arrangement might cool into silver rather than soap. Distance and time part bodies; longing keeps its own weather. Whether the beloved ever hears the spray is almost beside the point. The speaker remains on the edge of water, still calling, which is both devotion and a kind of chosen exile.
