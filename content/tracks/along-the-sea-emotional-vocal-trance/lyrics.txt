𝐿𝑖𝑘𝑒, 𝐶𝑜𝑚𝑚𝑒𝑛𝑡, 𝑆𝑢𝑏𝑠𝑐𝑟𝑖𝑏𝑒  ❤️🙏  

𝑳𝒚𝒓𝒊𝒄𝒔 🎶🎶  

Along the sea, I walked alone  
The wind would whisper through the foam  
Your name was carried on the tide  
A silent echo by my side  

The stars would burn above the waves  
Like ancient songs the silence saves  
And every shell beneath my feet  
Would tell of love I longed to meet  

Oh, where you are, I do not know  
But still I search, where waters flow  
Through endless blue, through dusk and day  
My heart will follow anyway  

The moonlight weeps upon the sand  
As if it, too, could understand  
How distance grows, how time can part  
But cannot silence longing’s heart  

So if you hear me in the spray  
A ghost of words the winds might say—  
Just know I walk, through dream and flame,  
Along the sea, still calling your name
