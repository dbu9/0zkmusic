I open my hands.  
I don’t ask.  

I don’t count.  
I don’t wait.  
I don’t trade love  
For fate.  

I step closer.  
I let go.  

Love is giving.  
Not receiving.  
No return.  
No believing.  

Love is light.  
Passed along.  
I give first.  
I stay strong.  

I give.  
I breathe.  

No debt.  
No claim.  
No need  
For a name.  

Love is giving.  
That is all.  
I don’t rise  
When you fall.  

I give.  
I’m free.
