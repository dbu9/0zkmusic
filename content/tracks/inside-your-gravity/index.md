## The idea

The room turns slowly, the walls breathe, and a shadow crosses the speaker’s skin. The person being addressed scarcely needs to enter. Their voice is already “behind my eyes,” a place the speaker would carry through any door. That makes the verse about the absence of chains especially unsettling. The door is unlocked, the speaker stays and asks for more, and the refrain still says leaving is impossible. All three things can be true within the gravity the song imagines.

Listen to what happens to the darkness near the end. At first it “feels like light,” as though the speaker had found a new way to see. By the last chorus it “feels like peace,” and the speaker has nothing left to prove. That change makes surrender sound almost restful. Then “I disappear” repeats in a voice that is still there to tell us about it, as if saying the words were the last act the speaker could call their own.
