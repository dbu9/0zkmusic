## The idea

A night companion neither fully human nor fully weather. Cold breath, trees dividing, hooves in mist, stars colliding; a whisper speaks the name; wind becomes flame. He rides beside, unseen yet near—offering peace and pain, love “beyond the rain.” Trance that is uplifting, driving, nocturnal can turn this into psychopomp music: escort through shadow fields and endless snow.

“The child inside my heart still cries, / The rider knows, he never lies.” Trust is absolute; destination is “where the lost ones go.” One may hear death, muse, memory, or inner double. The lyric prefers the chill of fidelity to explanation. What matters is being taken—not away from truth, but deeper into the weather that already knew your name.
