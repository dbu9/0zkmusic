## The idea

Distance without walls is a cruel geometry. The voice reaches through haze; nothing blocks the path, and still the other person stays far. What remains is the unsaid, echoing until silence itself starts to scream—louder, somehow, than any quarrel that might have settled the air.

Synthwave suits this ache: nocturnal chrome, a motor that keeps moving after the relationship has stalled. Trust was fire; now only sparks. “We were more than time could tether / But now there's only never” lands like a verdict spoken in a car at 2 a.m., headlights on empty road.

There is a late, quieter twist: the same silence that torments also proves the speaker was once truly known. Grief, if that is what this is, keeps that knowledge like a bruise you press to remember it is real.
