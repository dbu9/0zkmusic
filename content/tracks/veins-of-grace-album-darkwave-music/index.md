## The idea

An album framed as ritual between sacred and profane: grace in the veins, confession in ruins of faith and flesh. Titles sketch the stations—*Say No More*, *I Burn for You*, *Mocking Grace*, *My Guilt, My Name*, *Nocturn Sister*—guilt, desire, and the beauty of silence. Darkwave and synth-dark-pop as gospel for the over-loving and the fallen.

Nocturnal driving darkness suits a record that asks to be felt more than decoded. Analog warmth against synthetic decay: trembling hands still reaching. I take “veins of grace” as circulatory theology—salvation not pure, but still pumping.
