## Lyrics

Veins of Grace is a descent into the sacred and the profane — a sonic confession whispered in the ruins of faith and flesh. Blending dark electronic textures, spiritual melancholy, and raw emotional depth, this album explores the fragile line between salvation and surrender.  

Each track pulses with analog warmth and synthetic decay: distorted guitars bleed into ritualistic beats, ethereal synths echo like forgotten prayers, and intimate male vocals guide you through guilt, desire, and the unbearable beauty of silence.  

This is not just music — it’s a ritual. A gospel for the broken. A hymn for those who’ve loved too hard, fallen too far, and still dare to reach for grace with trembling hands.  

Veins of Grace invites you to feel… not to understand.
