## The idea

Demons that know your name, love that “feels the same,” highs that expire into lows that will not release you—the lyric stacks familiar wounds until they form a single pressure system. Heart torn, memories drowning, pills for fear that still sit in the chest: numetal/emo-rap bluntness, nocturnal and atmospheric rather than arena-loud.

The recurring question—*love or truth? love or the dark? love or a mistake?*—never quite resolves. Writing songs is admitted as failed medicine; ink cannot close the scar it describes. Escape is named in the title and denied in every chorus. What remains is the loop itself: fighting, chasing, losing pieces, asking a silence that already knows the answer.
