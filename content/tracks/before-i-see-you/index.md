## The idea

“Before I See You” follows unrequited longing as it becomes an organizing force in the speaker’s inner life. He often crosses paths with a woman he finds beautiful and cannot bring himself to approach her. Those recurring sightings give him a familiarity with her movements, so he begins to recognize her before she comes fully into view. The lyric gives that progression a physical form through rhythm, distorted perception, and finally the image of an orbit.

### A presence sensed in advance

The first glimpse is peripheral, and the detail that registers is a rhythm in the other person’s stride. The answering heartbeat supplies the first suggestion of a bond, entirely within the speaker’s own body. From there, the questions about the person’s secrets give way to questions about unlived lives and buried wishes. The attraction seems to have revived a longing older than the encounter. The paradox of a memory without a past gives that longing the emotional certainty of a recollection even though there is no shared history to recall.

### Gravity and unrequited recognition

The repeated “before” puts anticipation ahead of each act of perception. The speaker sees before looking, hears silence, and recognizes a voice whose owner does not know their name. These paradoxes convey the intimacy of the experience and establish how little of it is reciprocated. The line about gravity bending the speaker’s view prepares the star-and-planet metaphor. The distant star gives off light in every direction. The unnamed planet circles it, cold on the surface and burning within. An orbit gives that closeness a limit. The speaker can stay within the reach of the light without ever coming nearer to being known.

### An orbit out of sight

In the last refrain, feeling becomes burning and a disappearing world becomes a dissolving universe. The speaker can finally state the fact that has been implicit throughout. The other person never looks their way. The closing fragment still begins with the same act of anticipation, so resignation brings clarity without ending the fixation. In [Limerence (Original Mix)](../limerence-original-mix/), the other person’s departure lets the clock resume. Here the orbit continues after the speaker has understood their place in it.

[Silent Messager (Limerence 3)](../silent-messager/) gives the man’s unspoken feelings a witness.
