A thought appears.  
The air shifts.  
Light bends.  
Time drifts.  

Dreams unfold.  
The silence hums.  
Worlds awaken.  
The future comes.  

You think — it grows.  
You breathe — it flows.  
From mind to flame,  
All things have name.  

Shadows fall.  
The stars ignite.  
A wish becomes  
A thread of light.  

No sound.  
No form.  
Just will —  
Becoming storm.  

You think — it grows.  
You breathe — it flows.  
Within your thought,  
The cosmos glows.  

All begins.  
All ends.  
In thought.
