## Lyrics

In the glowing silence after love has burned away, this album rises — not in anger, but in memory.  

Ashes of the Beloved is a trance requiem for everything we believed in: love, virtue, friendship, humanity itself. Each track is a whispered elegy — a slow unraveling of sacred bonds, a meditation on the quiet places where faith once lived.  

Through ethereal synths, distant pulses, and lyrics carved in grief, the album weaves a delicate collapse — not dramatic, but intimate. The rhythms are slow-burning and melodic, like light bleeding through shattered glass. Voices drift like prayers that never reached the sky.  

This is not music for the dancefloor. It is music for the afterglow — for standing in ruins and breathing in the ash.  

For those who once believed. For those who still remember how it felt.
