She comes in the night  
No sound, no light  
I wait, I fall  
Into her call  

A breath, a flame  
She speaks my name  
No past, no pain  
Just dark remains  

Daughter of dusk  
Shadow and skin  
She draws me close  
She pulls me in  

Eyes like smoke  
Voice so thin  
She steals my sleep  
She crawls within  

No love, no rest  
Just her embrace  
I lose myself  
In her cold grace  

Daughter of dusk  
Forever near  
She feeds on dreams  
And swallows fear  

She doesn’t cry  
She doesn’t care  
She walks through fire  
Like empty air  

No stars, no dawn  
No final sign  
Only her kiss  
Outside of time
