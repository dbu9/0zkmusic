[Verse 1]  
How close the unattainable stands—  
one breath between, a life apart.  
You pass through ordinary hours;  
mine stop before they start.  
The shadow beneath your lashes,  
the pale half-moons upon your nails;  
the bronze where summer touched your skin,  
the white where summer pales.  
Your scent brings May into the cold.  
For one brief breath, the winter clears.  
I know the light upon your face—  
not what has brought you through the years.  
[Verse 2]  
Above me waited wind and light,  
a road beyond the stone.  
I stayed to share the air with you  
and knew I'd leave alone.  
For you, an hour like the rest.  
For me, a life held still.  
I choose the sweetness and its cost.  
I know I always will.  
[Pre-Chorus]  
I gave my word before we met.  
No hunger makes it break.  
There is a world I long to enter—  
and a step I will not take.  
[Chorus]  
Stay, passing hour—  
one breath, then let it end.  
I carry happiness like a wound  
I would not choose to mend.  
What I can never hold  
is what holds me here.  
No word. No step.  
A lifetime far—  
one breath near.  
[Bridge]  
Your silence never promised me  
a future or a name.  
The beauty's yours. The hunger's mine.  
We do not share the flame.  
No secret sign behind your eyes,  
no promise in the air.  
A world was born and died in me—  
you were only standing there.  
[Breakdown]  
No word.  
No claim.  
No one to blame.  
I want.  
I burn.  
I stay.  
One step—  
I will not take.  
One vow—  
I will not break.  
My happiness.  
My pain.  
Two names  
for the same flame.  
[Final Chorus]  
Stay, passing hour—  
one breath, then let it end.  
I carry happiness like a wound  
I would not choose to mend.  
What I can never hold  
is what holds me here.  
No word. No step.  
A lifetime far—  
one breath near.  
[Outro]  
You leave.  
The air is air again.  
The clock remembers time.  
The vow remains unbroken.  
The wound remains mine.
