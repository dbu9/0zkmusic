I walk,  
lights low,  
no place  
to go.  

One heart,  
one room,  
one ghost  
in bloom.  

I breathe  
your name,  
but no one  
came.  

I’m here,  
alone,  
far from  
home.  

Empty streets,  
empty sky,  
silent tears  
I never cry.  

Hold me close,  
let me fall,  
I feel nothing  
after all.  

I wait,  
lights fade,  
lost in  
the shade.  

One touch,  
one dream,  
too far  
from me.  

I breathe  
your name,  
but no one  
came.  

I’m here,  
alone,  
far from  
home.  

Empty streets,  
empty sky,  
silent tears  
I never cry.  

Hold me close,  
let me fall,  
I feel nothing  
after all.
