## The idea

This track is about the dangerous side of trust. The voice does not ask for a public confession, or for a truth that can be checked, judged, and forgiven in daylight. It asks for the secret at night, behind a quiet door, where there is no witness and no proof. "What you say, I will not betray" sounds like protection, but it also creates a private room that only two people can enter.

The repeated "vertrau dich mir" is tender because it offers shelter, but it is not innocent. A secret that "sleeps in me" becomes part of the other person's body; care begins to resemble possession. The promise to keep watch can mean devotion, but it can also mean that someone is now standing guard over the most vulnerable thing you have given away.

By the end, darkness is not defeated by explanation. It becomes wind because it has finally been spoken. The song understands confession as surrender: come closer, turn off the light, say the thing that frightens you, and trust that the one holding it will not use it to break you.
