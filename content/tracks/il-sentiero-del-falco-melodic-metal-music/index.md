## The idea

Alone in cold wind among extinguished voices, the walker seeks the hawk’s path—*il sentiero del falco*—beyond black sky and denied truth. Every word a wound; every breath a lost prayer. Melodic metal opens the space where flight becomes the only remaining answer.

The hawk flies without chains, between death and light, toward a sky that is finally its own. Italian vowels carry the grief; the riffs carry the ascent. Eternity is not comfort—it is what remains after life breaks in order to be reborn.
