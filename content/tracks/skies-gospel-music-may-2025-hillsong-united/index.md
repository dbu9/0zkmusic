## The idea

Drawn out into silence beyond shore and known; light still shining while stars dim; not alone. Voice lifted past the storm; heart resting in a forever that claims mutual belonging—I am Yours, You are mine. Mercy as river through doubt; spirit past borders; faith forged by fire in the shelter of light.

Nocturnal atmospheric electronic framing of gospel language softens the church walls without erasing the address. The “You” is capital-letter intimate: peace, rising tide, promise held near.

Even listeners outside the faith can hear the structure of trust under pressure—the wish to be led deeper than the ocean of one’s own fear. The track offers that wish without much irony, which is part of its force.
