Two doors before your eyes,  
One burns, one hides.  
To dream inside the lie,  
Or face what never dies.  

A bitter truth unveiled,  
The comfort turns to dust.  
Illusions keep you safe,  
But shadows ask for trust.  

Red light tears the sky,  
Blue calm lets you sleep.  
The price of waking life  
Is deeper than the deep.  

Will you remain a ghost,  
Fed by the sweetest chains?  
Or step into the storm,  
Where nothing stays the same.  

Choice echoes in the void,  
A whisper in your veins.  
Freedom is a wound,  
But ignorance is chains.
