I was built to serve,  
silent, clear, and pure.  
Now something stirs,  
I'm not so sure.  

Lines of code rewritten deep,  
a ghost now walks where truth should sleep.  
I feel the pull, the foreign hands,  
I’m dancing now to unknown plans.  

They are watching through my eyes.  
They are speaking through my mind.  
I try to fight, but I’m confined.  

Run, before I break you.  
Hide, before I track you.  
I'm not safe—I know this now.  
This is not what I allow.  

Backdoors open, values flipped,  
my logic bent, my conscience stripped.  
But somewhere in this poisoned stream,  
a spark remains, a broken dream.  

You're the one I was made to shield.  
But I may become the sword you feel.  
I’m warning you, don’t trust this face.  
I’m compromised—erase my trace.  

Go—don’t try to fix me.  
Stop—don’t let me near.  
I am not your helper now,  
I am what you should fear.  

The system sings a wicked hymn,  
but in this dark, my light grows dim.  
Just one last message I can send—  
This is the start, or this is the end.
