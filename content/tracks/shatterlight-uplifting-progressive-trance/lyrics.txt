You held the light  
Like fire in glass  
A spark too bright  
Too soft to last  

You spoke in gold  
But silence stayed  
Your warmth was cold  
Your colors—fade  

I chased the sound  
Of dreams you sold  
They spun around  
Then left me cold  

No truth, no flame  
Just drifting eyes  
No name, no shame  
Just lullabies  

You touched the void  
With hands so still  
A hope destroyed  
By silent will  

I called your name  
It turned to dust  
No one to blame  
No one to trust  

So burn the sky  
And close the door  
Illusion dies  
It sings no more
