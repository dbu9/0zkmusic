Ash in the sky.  
Stars pass by.  

Grain in the sand.  
No place to stand.  

Time runs deep.  
Nothing to keep.  

Cosmos is blind.  
Leaves us behind.  

Only the flame.  
Memory’s name.  

If they forget,  
We fade to net.  

If they recall,  
We live in all.  

We are the breath.  
We are not death.  

We are the song.  
Memory strong.
