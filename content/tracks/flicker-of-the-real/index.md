## The idea

A steadying address: silence will not break you; cradled shadows were never meant to author you. The void does not truly answer—the sought voice still lives in the mind. Brokenness is refused as final identity even when the world pretends otherwise.

Shattered mirrors were only borrowed sorrow, youth spent on flickers. The speaker has seen the storm in another’s gaze, the fight inside silence. “Flicker of the real” names a person as the reason dawn learns feeling—not fiction, not pure light, something intermittent and true. Dark-uplifting trance can hold that tenderness without softening it into slogan.
