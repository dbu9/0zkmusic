She wasn’t light,  
She was the wound.  
A perfect shape  
Carved from the ruin.  

She moved like grief,  
She breathed despair.  
But I saw stars  
Inside her stare.  

Not love.  
Not warmth.  
But something pure.  
A spiral down  
I would endure.  

I kissed her shadow,  
Tasted flame.  
She whispered hunger—  
And took my name.  

She was the trap  
That knew my path,  
The silent laugh  
Behind the glass.  

I loved her like  
A moth loves fire.  
Drawn to burn,  
My dark desire.  

She held no promise,  
Only fall.  
But in that fall—  
I felt it all.  

Not pain.  
Not fear.  
But perfect rest.  
The void,  
The pull  
Inside my chest.  

She isn't dream,  
She isn’t lie.  
She’s what remains  
When angels die.  

And if I follow,  
If I drown—  
I wear her dark  
Like royal crown.
