## The idea

Ethics of the unruled. No judge, no king, no hand to bless—only power, stress, and a walk that writes its own fate. Heart black, mind cold, trust dead; loyalty a ghost that plays with heads.

Rap’s drive carries the creed without blushing: be strong, be cold, or watch it fall. Mercy is declined not as philosophy seminar but as survival math. Whether you hear free will or pure predation, the unjudged stand outside grace on purpose.
