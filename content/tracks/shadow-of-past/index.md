## The idea

The song begins by addressing someone directly. “You are the shadow of past” could be said to a person who survives in memory, or to the past itself after it has taken on that person’s shape. The speaker tries to grasp a voice as though it were something the hands could hold. Then silence burns in the veins. The memory keeps slipping beyond reach while its effects are felt as close as the body.

Something similar happens when the broken dreams “whisper but never reply.” A whisper can sound intimate enough to make you lean closer. A reply would require someone on the other side who could hear you. The chorus keeps returning to the same fading echo, and the last two lines bring back the “you” of the opening with one change. The shadow lasts. Everything that once supplied its shape can turn to dust, and the speaker still has someone to address.
