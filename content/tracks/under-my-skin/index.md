## The idea

The speaker bites their tongue early in the song, and the pain goes on making itself felt in places no one else can see. They build walls to keep control, then feel those walls closing in. Even the face they show the world is something they “wear.” For a moment that face sounds like another layer of skin, useful for getting through a day while something underneath struggles to survive.

The first choruses describe a fight that makes its own conditions worse. Every time the speaker pushes, whatever hurts sinks deeper. The bridge begins with a demand for the noise to get out. By the time the speaker says “I won’t become what’s in my head,” they’re making a promise to themself. In the last chorus something is still breaking, and the scars still register. When the pressure eases, the lyric gives us a modest measure of what has changed. The speaker can feel those scars without letting them decide who they are.
