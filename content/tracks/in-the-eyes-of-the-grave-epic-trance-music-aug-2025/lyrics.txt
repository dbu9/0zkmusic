What thou dost build,  
Shall fall to dust.  
What thou dost love,  
Shall fade, as must.  

Gold turns to sand,  
Crowns turn to clay.  
The grave makes kings,  
And slaves the same.  


Pulvis et umbra sumus,  
Tempus fugit, mors certa.  
Ubi sunt qui ante nos fuerunt?  
In aeternum, silentium.  


Fair face shall pale,  
Proud form shall bend.  
Beauty is brief,  
Ash is the end.  

All songs are hushed,  
All fires wane.  
The earth reclaims,  
The dark shall reign.  


Pulvis et umbra sumus,  
Tempus fugit, mors certa.  
Ubi sunt qui ante nos fuerunt?  
In aeternum, silentium.  


Where are they now,  
The ones before?  
Their names are wind,  
Their crowns no more.  

So draw thy breath,  
So spend thy flame.  
For in the grave,  
All is the same.  


Pulvis et umbra sumus,  
Tempus fugit, mors certa.  
In aeternum, silentium…
