The night is deep, my soul is torn.  
I wait for you, though hope is worn.  

The moonlight weeps, the stars grow still.  
I chase a dream I cannot fill.  

I hold the past, it slips away.  
I lose the dawn, I lose the day.  

Your touch is gone, your breath is far.  
Yet still I pray to where you are.  

The sea is black, the waves confide.  
I walk alone without your side.  

I call your name, the silence near.  
The wind returns what none can hear.  

A flame once burned, it now is cold.  
But in my heart it takes its hold.  

The night recalls what time erased.  
I seek your eyes I cannot face.  

Your shadow moves, it haunts the ground.  
I hear your steps, without a sound.  

The sky is vast, the stars are frail.  
I write your name, but words will fail.  

Through broken glass, I see you clear.  
The world dissolves, but you are near.  

My heart is bound, my fate is cast.  
I live for you, though love has passed.  

The hour is late, the clock is blind.  
I search for what I’ll never find.
