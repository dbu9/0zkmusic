## The idea

From smoke and steel: a shadow, a mark. He comes, screams, hunts, remains. Chains sing, thunder breaks; a spirit of wars, a cold light. No end, no death—only fire, only blood. In fog and horror he lurks; earth shakes; walls fall; eye burns deaf to noise. He whispers night, war, death, victory. White tiger without victory, eternal war; from flames he rises, in flames he is silent. No sword, no ban, no human, no plan—eternal enemy, war made flesh.

Aggressive intense metal-electro can turn such fragments into a single white glare. The tiger is less animal than principle: war as presence that cannot be defeated because it is not fully of the human order.

I hear myth stripped to incantation. Whether nationalist ghost, pure nightmare, or metaphor for unkillable conflict, the track refuses soft edges. The white tiger does not negotiate.
