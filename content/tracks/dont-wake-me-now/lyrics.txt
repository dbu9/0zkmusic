White hands.  
White bed.  
You mourn a man  
Who is not dead.  

My eyes are closed.  
My breath is slow.  
You cannot see  
Where I now go.  



Don’t wake me now.  
Don’t call me gone.  
I found a world  
That needs no dawn.  

Your sun is pale.  
Your sky is cold.  
Inside of me,  
The stars are gold.  



Your world was made  
By every mind.  
Each left a shade.  
All shades combined.  

A million dreams  
Became one grey.  
I kept one flame  
And slipped away.  



Don’t wake me now.  
Don’t call me gone.  
I found a world  
That needs no dawn.  

Your sun is pale.  
Your sky is cold.  
Inside of me,  
The stars are gold.  



They take my grief  
Through tubes and wire.  
They leave me thought  
And wordless fire.  

No hand can touch.  
No eye can see  
The final world  
That dies with me.  



Don’t wake me now.  
Don’t call me gone.  
My silent world  
Still carries on.  

Your daylight fades.  
Your heavens flee.  
But all the stars  
Still burn in me.  



White hands.  
White bed.  
A world still lives  
Inside the dead.
