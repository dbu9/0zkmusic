## The idea

“White hands. / White bed.” The song opens on what someone standing at the bedside can see. The voice belongs to the man being mourned, and what he sees is a sky full of gold stars. The lyric draws on the ending of the Russian science-fiction story “Предыдущий часовой пояс” (“The Previous Time Zone”), which imagines a shared reality made by blending countless private visions. Each mind contributes a shade, and all those shades together make grey. The man keeps one flame for himself and slips away with it.

He finds a world that “needs no dawn,” so the usual promise of waking up has little to offer him. Still, one of his last thoughts is that no hand can touch the world he has made. Its colors seem inseparable from the solitude in which he found them. When the white hands and white bed return at the end, the lyric has changed “Who is not dead” to “Inside the dead.” The image at the bedside is the same. By then we have spent the song in the place no one at that bedside can see, the world that will die with him.
