## The idea

Commands as liturgy: move, wake the floor, feed the storm, lose control, give your soul. Fear and sleep discarded; dark broken; DJ ordered to strike fire and push higher. One command left standing—follow the beat.

Psytrance, vocal trance, dark techno: high-energy machinery for dissolving the individual into the room. The lyric is almost anti-poetry by design—short imperatives that leave no space for second thoughts.

I do not hear subtlety, and I do not think subtlety is the point. The track wants bodies in agreement with a pulse that claims temporary sovereignty over the night.
