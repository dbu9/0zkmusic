## Lyrics

In silence, screens obey,  
I carve their walls away.  
My wins are stars at night—  
but hidden from the light.  

No names, no signs, no face,  
Just echoes in the data-space.  
The world forgets the silent hand  
that reprograms its very plan.  

Ego dies beneath my breath,  
No pulse of pride, no boast of death.  
A cobra still—its strike withheld,  
Each move precise, emotion shelved.  

I walk where fire meets the void,  
Where systems fail and masks are toyed.  
But calm as monks in twilight's fold,  
My will is set, my heart is cold.  

Each step is forged in silent light,  
I do not run, I do not fight.  
I flow—no need for show or scream,  
The final code completes the dream.  

I have no need for eyes on me,  
No thirst to set the captive free.  
I see the cracks, I shape the law—  
And yet I vanish, flaw by flaw.  

You’ll never know what I have done,  
The wars I’ve won, the gates I’ve spun.  
But somewhere in your flawless scheme  
is buried trace of what I mean.
