## The idea

Someone has left, but departure has not cleared the room. Breath remains in the walls and chest; a face recedes under snow; touch collapses into light and haze. “Afterimage” lives in that interval where the eyes know an absence before the body accepts it.

The lyric keeps reaching and finding only residue: shape in the air, taste before dawn, memory without proof. Berlin-school trance is a natural vessel for this suspended state, letting repetition behave like the mind returning to the same impossible question—was it real, and was it mine? Nothing stays, yet the trace keeps moving long after its source is gone.
