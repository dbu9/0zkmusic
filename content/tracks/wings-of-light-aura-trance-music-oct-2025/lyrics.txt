Whisper to me,  
Under the veil of dawn.  
Shadows fall,  
And the stars are gone.  

Breathe in the tide,  
Silver and slow —  
Carry my soul  
Where the soft winds go.  

Aaaah... elora...  
Light within the sky…  
Eyaaah... seluna…  
Dreams that never die.  

Through crystal rain,  
Through the quiet sea,  
Echoes of time  
Return to me.  

Flow, river of night,  
Shine, memory bright.  
Rise, spirit unseen,  
Awake within the dream.  

Aaaah... elora...  
Light within the sky…  
Eyaaah... seluna…  
Dreams that never die.  
Aaaah... elora…  
Guide me home again.
