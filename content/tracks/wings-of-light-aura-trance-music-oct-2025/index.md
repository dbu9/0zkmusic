## The idea

Whisper under dawn’s veil; shadows fall; stars gone; breathe silver tide and carry the soul where soft winds go. Invented names—*elora*, *seluna*—work like mantras more than characters: light in the sky, dreams that never die.

Crystal rain, quiet sea, river of night, spirit unseen waking inside the dream. Trance’s dark-uplift drive makes the aura physical. Guide me home again: the plea is simple under the glossolalia.

I take it as soft mysticism—less doctrine than floating toward brightness with wings the music supplies.
