## The idea

Alone on endless tide, calling a name through silent night; echoes in cold waves as the other slips away. Shadow lingers; stars fade into nothing; sky breaks into blue—and still the vow: I will be here, waiting, lost inside of you. Hands that were never there; ghost; only a name.

Dark-uplifting driving trance often frames longing as ascent. Here ascent is circular: every lift returns to the same interior captivity. “Lost inside of you” is both complaint and chosen address.

I hear attachment that has outlived presence—loyalty to an absence so total it becomes landscape. The song does not solve it; it only keeps the waiting audible.
