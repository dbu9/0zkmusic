## The idea

Sandstorm as sacred weather: dance in the air, golden waves, desert scent drifting through time. The Khamsin is named and freed—wind full of echoes “like waves on the sea,” crimson horizon as “gateway untold.” No sorrow, no shadow, “just light made of gold.”

Emotional trance often needs a landscape large enough for feeling; here the landscape *is* the feeling—weightless twilight, the moment flowing. *It’s sandstorm* becomes both description and invocation, a rhythm calling itself divine illusion.

I hear rapture without plot: the body agreeing to be weather for a few minutes of gold dust and glow.
