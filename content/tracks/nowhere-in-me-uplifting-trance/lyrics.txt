The days blur into static  
The sky hums out of tune  
I speak, but air is hollow  
Like shouting at the moon  

I walk in borrowed places  
With names that feel untrue  
A shadow draped in silence  
A ghost the world once knew  

I sleep where I don’t settle  
I dream what’s not my own  
This soil rejects my heartbeat  
These stars are not my home  

I’m drawn toward horizons  
Where colors never fade  
A call beyond the silence  
A map I’ve never made  

No flag, no final anchor  
No oath to hold me down  
I chase the wind’s direction  
I live to leave this town  

The road is sharp with freedom  
The end is far from clear  
But movement is my language  
And forward is sincere
