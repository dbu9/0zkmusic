## The idea

A spiral’s core is motion that never quite returns to the same place. Progressive trance, uplifting and driving, can sketch that geometry without a lyric to explain it—pulse as argument, ascent as unfinished question. What the title promises, the body of the track must prove in pressure and release alone.
