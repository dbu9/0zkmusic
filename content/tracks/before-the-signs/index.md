## The idea

These lines try to speak from under the threshold of intention. “Before the signal / Before the sound / Something stirs / Without a ground”—emergence without a commander, membranes and systems rearranging before consciousness can claim authorship. Trance’s uplifting drive suits that paradox: structure appearing as if from nowhere while the body already moves to it.

The lyric insists it is not ordinary metaphor: life “small as a sigh,” learning the world “by being close,” present “before the signs.” Perhaps that is nanotechnology, or spirit, or the moment a new self organizes in “broken men.” Borders and lines are what the eye seeks; the song prefers what was already flowing—“it stays / it flows.” The emotional charge is quiet awe at scale so fine that control vanishes, and beauty arrives without asking to be named.
