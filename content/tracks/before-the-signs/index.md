## Lyrics

This is a full trance track about nano-science and emergence.  

The lyrics reflect processes that occur below perception: cellular interaction, self-organization, and the appearance of structure without intention.  
Nothing here is metaphorical in the usual sense — the text follows how systems behave when scale becomes small enough that control disappears.  

Nano-scale imagery accompanies the track not as decoration, but as the same idea expressed visually: membranes forming, systems rewiring, matter reorganizing itself.  

No command.  
No design.  
Only emergence.
