## The idea

Blood as weather, not romance. The ground is silent yet keeps the trace; no light, no plan—only something that “rolls” and pulls. Hemoglobin “pulls, it sinks / where no one drinks”: life-fluid turned predatory or simply indifferent, finding you whether you cry out or not. Atmospheric death metal’s aggression fits a forest of mute trees and cold steel, a quiet vow in deep woods.

“It doesn’t breathe, / Yet it will bite.” The horror is systemic rather than theatrical—no shield works, no step escapes. One can hear environmental dread, ancestral blood-debt, or the body’s own chemistry as stalker. The refrain will not forget you. In that refusal of mercy the lyric finds its cold beauty: the world yields no dialogue, only the slow certainty of what moves under the skin of everything.
