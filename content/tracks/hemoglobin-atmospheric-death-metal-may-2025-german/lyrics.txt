*Hemoglobin*  

The ground is silent  
Yet holds the trace  
No light, no sound  
Just ancient pace  

No call, no plan  
It rolls, it spans  

Hemoglobin —  
It pulls, it sinks  
Hemoglobin —  
Where no one drinks  

The trees are mute  
The steel is cold  
A quiet vow  
A forest old  

No shield, no cry  
But it goes by  

Hemoglobin —  
It pulls, it sinks  
Hemoglobin —  
Where no one drinks  

"It doesn’t breathe,  
Yet it will bite.  
No step escapes —  
It finds by night."  

Hemoglobin —  
No word, no light  
Hemoglobin —  
Won’t lose your sight
