## The idea

Blake’s binary—some born to sweet delight, some to endless night—gets dragged into street grit and class anger. Rap cadence suits the argument: dice that aren’t fair, kings breathing poisoned air, rich mouths at a glass while others beg for a piece of the past.

The chorus is almost liturgical in its cruelty: some get crowns, some get scars; some get light, some burn in the dark. Fate is neither kind nor merciful; the speaker runs the maze like a ghost and watches luck set fire to someone else’s stage. Prayer goes to the moon because there is no sun in this sky.

I hear less self-pity than inventory. The track names the distribution of pain without promising redistribution—only the cold clarity of knowing which night you were born into.
