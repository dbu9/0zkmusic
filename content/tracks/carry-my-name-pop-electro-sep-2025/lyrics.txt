When the night turns hollow,  
And the stars forget to shine,  
I will wait in silence,  
For your heart to answer mine.  

Every breath is fragile,  
Like a prayer lost in the wind,  
Still I guard the embers,  
Where our shadows once had been.  

Carry my name across the dark,  
Through the storm, through the endless spark.  
If the world should fade away,  
I will still be here, I will stay.  
Carry my name, don’t let it die,  
Let it burn in the endless sky.  
Even if the distance grows,  
My soul remembers, my soul still knows.  

I have written letters,  
On the waves that drift to shore.  
Though the sea consumes them,  
I will write a thousand more.  

Time may break its promise,  
But it cannot take my flame.  
Even if the night forgets,  
You will always hear my name.  

Carry my name across the dark,  
Through the storm, through the endless spark.  
If the world should fade away,  
I will still be here, I will stay.  
Carry my name, don’t let it die,  
Let it burn in the endless sky.  
Even if the distance grows,  
My soul remembers, my soul still knows.
