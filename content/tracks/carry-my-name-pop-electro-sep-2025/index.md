## The idea

Night turns hollow; stars forget how to shine; still someone waits for an answer that may never arrive. The plea is simple and enormous: carry my name across the dark, through storm and spark, so that even if the world fades the name does not die. Letters written on waves that the sea will eat; a thousand more written anyway.

Dark electro-pop can make devotion sound cinematic. Here devotion is also anxiety—distance growing, flame guarded in silence where shadows once were. “My soul remembers, my soul still knows” is less triumph than vow spoken against erasure.

I take it as love that has already lost the easy nearness and is fighting for residual presence: not possession of the body, but persistence of the name in another’s weather.
